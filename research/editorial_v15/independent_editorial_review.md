## Findings requiring correction

1. **Medium — declaration deduplication is overstated.** `report_body.md:112` says “không lặp lại khai báo đã xét”. Frozen `scripts/prepare_groq_full_scope.py:40–48` tracks `seen` **names**, not declaration-node identity. Multi-name assignments can be appended repeatedly. The saved manifest contains duplicate top-level declarations in three closures: `sanic__sanic.headers__parse_content_header`, `sanic__sanic.headers__format_http1_response`, and `sanic__sanic.headers__parse_forwarded`. For example, the assignment to `_token, _quoted` appears twice in the first closure. Suggested wording: “không xét lại cùng một tên; một khai báo gán nhiều tên có thể xuất hiện lặp”. Describe the implementation that generated the evidence; do not repair frozen closures.

2. **Medium — inconclusive evidence is not falsification.** `scripts/v15_research_tables.py:8`, column “Kết quả có thể bác bỏ kỳ vọng”, includes “Khoảng bất định không xác lập ưu thế”. Failure to establish superiority does not refute a beneficial effect. Rename that column to “Kết quả làm hạn chế bằng chứng cho kỳ vọng”, or distinguish contradiction from insufficient precision. Likewise, observing mixed project directions does not by itself refute a positive average effect. The body correctly makes this distinction at `report_body.md:193`.

## Small precision improvements

- **Probe policy, body:117 / tables:10:** the broad description is consistent, but an exact rule list would improve reproducibility: skip defaulted and variadic parameters; reject required positional-only parameters; callback-name set `{f, fn, func, function, callback, mapper}` gets `lambda *args: 1`; names starting `is_` or annotations satisfying **identity** `p.annotation is bool` get `False`; names `{errors, items, values, rules}` get `[]`; everything else gets `1`. These are one fixed keyword-argument assignment, not a candidate search. String annotations such as `'bool'` do not satisfy that identity test. Precedence is callback, Boolean, list, fallback. Existing prose does not explicitly claim broader type inference, so this is clarification, not a demonstrated numerical error. Source: frozen preparation script:10–21.
- **Closure character budget, body:112 / tables:10:** 8,000 is the sum of extracted declaration lengths; the inserted `\n\n` separators are not counted. Describe this as a declaration-content budget if claiming exact serialized bounds. No actual saved closure exceeds 8,000 characters (maximum serialized length 7,897), so the reported experiment is unaffected.
- **Primary estimand, tables:9:** “Tỷ lệ cân bằng dự án” is an arm-level summary; the prespecified primary estimand is the equal-project **difference C−A**. Label arm rates as supporting summaries and the primary contrast explicitly. Body:186–193 already identifies the contrast correctly. Coverage remains descriptive, paired turnover/failure stages/subgroups/seed sensitivity exploratory post hoc, and packs supplementary exploratory.
- **Design inference, body:184:** “sẽ dẫn tới kết luận cần cải thiện tính độc lập trạng thái” is stronger than the evidence identifies. Prefer “gợi ý kiểm tra tính độc lập trạng thái và điều kiện tích hợp”. Pack size, order and resources are alternative explanations. Body:272 already uses the appropriately tentative formulation.

## Editorial balance

Retain the full statistical interpretation once in §4.2 and a concise final answer in §5.1; §4.6 can focus on the distinct validity issues. Test4Py non-comparability also recurs in §§1.3, 3.5, 4.6, 4.7 and 5.2; centralize its detailed explanation in design/limitations.

Lead synthesis with the measured contribution: complete paired component evaluation over the eligibility frame; bidirectional acceptance turnover; the pooled/balanced reversal; reproducible accepted outputs; and the standalone/integration tradeoff. Then state the uncertainty and transfer limits once. No need to strengthen superiority claims or add more disclaimers. The current distinction between evidence of feasibility, exploratory mechanisms and unestablished general superiority is sound.
