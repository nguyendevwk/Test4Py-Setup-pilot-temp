# Scientific protocol and fairness

The current operational rules are in the parent SKILL.md and
`docs/MULTITARGET_SMOKE.md`. Historical case-study numbers are not universal claims.

- Council without setup is not Test4Py upstream.
- Two PyCG mutants are two illustrations, not a broad fault-detection benchmark.
- Report module and target/nested coverage separately.
- Stochastic seeds within one target are repeated attempts, not independent targets.
- No N, temperature or test procedure guarantees a desired p-value.
- Lock hypotheses, target selection, revisions, budgets and handling of missingness
  before evaluating held-out projects. Report paired effects with uncertainty.
- Preserve failures, generated assertions and all repairs. Do not curate successful
  seeds or filter failed tests into a misleading all-pass headline.
- Probe success proves only the observed execution under that environment.
- API-free inference still consumes compute. Report GPU-hours and stage costs.
- Claims of efficacy require matched controls and reviewed oracles; screen success
  alone does not prove improvements over the paper.
